# 📞 I Dialer

<img alt="Logo" src="app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_launcher.png" width="120" />

**Welcome to I Dialer – a smarter, sleeker way to connect!**

I Dialer is a modern, open-source dialer app for Android that reimagines how you manage calls and messages. It's more than just a dialer—it's your communication hub.

✨ **Features at a Glance**  
- Instantly manage your **call history** and **contacts**  
- Make calls or send messages with ease—**integrated with WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Viber, and Threema**  
- Built-in **AI-powered spam detection** to keep you safe  

We're actively developing I Dialer and are looking for passionate collaborators! Whether you're a designer, developer, tester, or just someone with great ideas—we’d love to have you on board.

🤝 **Let’s build the future of dialing together!**
